How to set a simple Thanksgiving table with printables

  1. Print the place cards on cardstock and write each guest's name.
  2. Put a gratitude card and a pencil at every seat.
  3. Hang the printable banner or garland over the table or the buffet.
  4. After dinner, read the gratitude cards out loud. It's the best part of the night.
