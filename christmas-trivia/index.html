Christmas trivia that works for a mixed crowd

Trivia is the party game that needs the least setup: one person reads, everyone else argues. It fits the lull after dinner, a classroom party on the last day before break, an office lunch or a video call with family in another state. The trick is questions that grandparents and teens can both answer, and an answer key so nobody has to look things up mid-game.

How to run a Christmas trivia game

  1. Print the question sheet and answer key on plain US Letter or A4 paper at "Actual size".
  2. Split into teams of 3 to 5 and give each team a pen and scrap paper. Mix ages so kids and adults help each other.
  3. Read each question twice, give 30 seconds, then reveal answers every 5 questions so scores stay exciting.
  4. Finish with the Emoji Carol Quiz as a bonus round: double points if the team sings the first line.
  5. Prizes: candy canes, hot cocoa packets, or first pick at the cookie tray.
