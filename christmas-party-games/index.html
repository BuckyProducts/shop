Printable Christmas party games that work for any crowd

The best Christmas party games need no setup, no app and no extra shopping: print one sheet per player, hand out pens and start. That's why printable games work so well for family nights, classroom parties, office get-togethers and church events.

  • Christmas charades: great for mixed ages. Cut the cards, fold them into a bowl and let teams act out a reindeer, a snowman or wrapping a present.
  • Christmas Would You Rather: an easy icebreaker for office parties and teens. No winners or losers, just good conversation.
  • Christmas Scattergories: pick a letter and fill in a Christmas food, song or gift in under a minute. Works for 3 to 30 players.
  • Word searches and crosswords: a quiet option for grandparents and kids who'd rather sit out the loud games. The large-print versions are easy on the eyes.
  • Gift exchange games: pair a White Elephant or Secret Santa round with printable rules so nobody argues about stealing.

How to run a printable game night

  1. Download the PDF and print on US Letter or A4 plain paper. Cardstock is nice for charades cards you'll reuse.
  2. Print one game sheet per player or per team, plus a few spares.
  3. Set a timer on your phone (60 to 90 seconds per round keeps the energy up).
  4. Have a small prize ready: a candy cane, a mug or first pick at the gift exchange.

Every game comes in English and Spanish, so bilingual families and dual-language classrooms can play together.
