Printable Christmas gift tags: why they beat store-bought

A pack of foil tags runs out the night you need it. Printable tags don't: download the PDF once and print another sheet whenever the pile of presents grows. They also match, so the whole stack under the tree looks planned even if you wrapped it at midnight.

How to print and attach gift tags

  1. Download the PDF (the free mini kit comes with 8 tags, the Christmas Cards set with 16).
  2. Print on white cardstock, 65 to 110 lb, at "Actual size" so the tags keep their shape. Plain paper works too if you glue the tag onto the package instead of hanging it.
  3. Cut along the lines with scissors or a paper trimmer. A round hole punch at the top corner turns any tag into a hanging tag.
  4. Thread twine, ribbon or baker's string through the hole, or use a strip of double-sided tape to stick the tag flat on the paper.

Quick ideas

  • Teacher and neighbor gifts: pair a tag with cookies, a candle or a mug and you have a gift in five minutes.
  • Secret Santa and gift exchanges: write the number or code on the back so nobody peeks before the reveal.
  • Bilingual families: the tags come in English and Spanish, so you can write "Merry Christmas" for one side of the family and "Feliz Navidad" for the other.
  • Matching cards: the Christmas Cards set includes 12 folding cards with the same look as its tags.
