How to print and fold a quarter-fold card

  1. Download the PDF and print on white cardstock (65 to 110 lb) at "Actual size". The cards print on one side only, so there is no double-sided setting to fight with.
  2. Fold the sheet in half the long way, then in half again, so the picture ends up on the front.
  3. Run a thumbnail or a ruler along each fold for a crisp edge.
  4. Sign inside, slip it into an A2 envelope and add a matching gift tag to the present.

What size are the cards?

Each card folds down to 4.25 x 5.5 inches and fits a standard A2 envelope. The A4 version folds to about 10.5 x 14.8 cm.

Do I need to print double-sided?

No. The cards print on one side of the sheet; you fold the page in four and the message lands inside.

What paper should I use?

White cardstock, 65 to 110 lb, gives the best result. Plain printer paper works for a quick card.
