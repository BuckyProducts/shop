Why large-print puzzles help

Regular puzzle books use small type and crowded grids, which turns a relaxing activity into eye strain. Large-print puzzles use big, bold letters and roomy grids, so readers with low vision, older adults and anyone who is tired of squinting can solve comfortably. Because these are printables, you can print one page or the whole book, as many times as you like, and make the pages even bigger by printing at "Fit to page" on US Letter.

Which puzzles are inside

  • 100 Large Print Puzzles for Seniors: word searches, crosswords, sudoku and mazes in large print, with every answer included. You get a complete English book and a complete Spanish book.
  • Large Print Christmas Puzzles: 20 holiday word searches and 15 crosswords with answers, in English and Spanish. A good stocking stuffer for a grandparent or a quiet activity for Christmas afternoon.

Ideas for using them

  • Gifts for grandparents: print the set, punch holes and put it in a binder with a pen and a highlighter. It's a thoughtful gift you can make the night before a visit.
  • Senior centers, assisted living and memory care: activity coordinators can print a fresh stack for every group session. Pick easier word searches for some residents and sudoku for others.
  • Caregivers: a puzzle page is a calm, screen-free activity for waiting rooms, recovery days and quiet afternoons.
  • Family time: solve a crossword together across generations. The bilingual books also work for families where grandparents prefer Spanish.

How to print large-print puzzles at home

  1. Download the PDF right after checkout.
  2. Print on plain US Letter or A4 paper. Choose "Fit to page" to make the type as big as possible.
  3. Use a dark felt-tip pen or marker instead of a pencil, since bold lines are easier to see.
  4. Keep the answer pages aside so nobody peeks.
