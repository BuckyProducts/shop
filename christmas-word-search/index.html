Why a printable Christmas word search works so well

A word search is the one activity almost everyone can do: kids who just learned to read, teens killing time on winter break, and grandparents who want something calm after dinner. Print a page, hand over a pencil and the table goes quiet for twenty minutes.

  • Family gatherings: leave a stack of word searches next to the coffee so guests have something to do while the turkey or the ham finishes.
  • Classrooms: a Christmas word search is an easy early-finisher page in the last week before break, and the answer key makes checking fast.
  • Seniors and memory care: large print and roomy grids are easier on the eyes, and a familiar holiday theme makes the words easier to find.
  • Travel and waiting rooms: fold a page into a bag for the airport, the car or the doctor's office.

Make it a game

  1. Print one copy of the same puzzle for every player.
  2. Set a timer for five or ten minutes. Whoever finds the most words wins a candy cane.
  3. For mixed ages, let younger kids team up with an adult, or give the adults a handicap of one minute.
  4. Use the answer key at the end to settle any arguments.

Printing tips

Print on plain US Letter or A4 paper at "Actual size". Use a pencil so pages can be checked against the answer key, or slip a page into a sheet protector and use a dry-erase marker to reuse it all season.
