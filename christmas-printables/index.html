Christmas printables for every part of the season

  • Party games: charades, Would You Rather and Christmas Scattergories work for family nights, classrooms and office parties. Print one copy per player and you're ready.
  • Cards and gift tags: print on cardstock, fold and write a name. It's the fastest way to finish wrapping on December 23.
  • Quiet-time activities: word searches, crosswords and coloring pages keep kids busy on winter break and give grandparents a large-print option.
  • Planning ahead: a 2027 planner and calendar help you start January organized.

How to print them at home

  1. Download the PDF right after checkout (or right away for the free kit).
  2. Print on US Letter or A4. Use plain paper for games and puzzles, and cardstock (65 to 110 lb) for cards and tags.
  3. Choose "Actual size" or "Fit to page" in your printer settings.
  4. Cut along the lines, and you're done. Print as many copies as you need for your own home or classroom.

Every file is bilingual (English and Spanish), so it works for dual-language classrooms and bilingual families too.
