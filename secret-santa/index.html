How to run a Secret Santa that actually works

  1. Set the budget and the date. Agree on one price limit for everyone ($10, $15, $20 or $25 are the most common) and the day you'll swap gifts.
  2. Hand out wish lists. Each person fills in a short list: favorite color, snack, hobby, size and one thing they don't want. It saves everyone from guessing.
  3. Draw names. Write each name on a draw card, fold it and put them all in a bowl or hat. If someone pulls their own name, they put it back and draw again.
  4. Keep it secret. Label each gift with a printable tag that shows only the recipient's name, so the giver stays a mystery until the reveal.
  5. Reveal. At the party, everyone guesses who their Secret Santa was before opening.

Secret Santa gift ideas by budget

  • Under $10: hot cocoa and a mug, fun socks, a scented candle, a small succulent, a deck of cards or a box of favorite candy.
  • Under $15: an insulated tumbler, a coffee or tea sampler, a cozy throw-size blanket, a puzzle or a nice notebook and pen.
  • Under $25: a small Bluetooth speaker, a spa or bath kit, LED string lights, a board game or a plant with a pretty pot.

Office, family and classroom tips

  • Office: keep gifts neutral and set the budget in the invite. A printed wish list helps coworkers who barely know each other.
  • Family: draw names after Thanksgiving dinner so everyone has a month to shop.
  • Classroom: keep the budget low ($5 to $10) and let kids decorate their own tags.
  • Long distance: email the draw results privately and mail gifts with the printed tag inside.

Questions

What is a good Secret Santa budget?

Most groups choose $10 to $25. What matters most is that everyone agrees on the same limit before the draw.

How do you draw names for Secret Santa?

Write every name on a card, fold them into a bowl and have each person draw one in private. If someone draws their own name, they put it back and draw again. The Secret Santa Kit includes printable draw cards.

What should I give a coworker I don't know well?

Something useful and neutral: a mug with cocoa, a candle, a tumbler, a plant or a nice notebook.
