Thanksgiving games the whole table can play

The turkey takes hours, and the gap between "everyone's here" and "dinner's ready" is where printable games earn their keep. These work for a big family dinner, a Friendsgiving with roommates, or a classroom party the day before break.

  • Thanksgiving picture bingo: 20 different cards with a picture and a word on every square, plus 30 calling cards, so kids who can't read yet play alongside grandparents.
  • Thanksgiving trivia: 20 family-friendly questions with an answer key. Split the room into two teams and keep score on a napkin.
  • Who Am I? and charades: 24 cards for a game that needs nothing but the cards and a little courage.
  • Thanksgiving Scattergories: a game sheet with 12 categories and 3 rounds. Print one per player.
  • The Great Pie-Off: a sign, 8 star-rating ballots, 6 entry cards and 6 awards (Best in Show, Flakiest Crust, People's Choice and more) turn dessert into a contest.

Gratitude activities and a kids' table that runs itself

Put a stack of "I'm thankful for" cards (16 with prompts) by each plate and read them out before the pie. The gratitude tree poster comes with 18 leaves to color and stick on. For the kids' table there's an activity mat with tic-tac-toe, count-and-write, draw your plate and a turkey to color, plus a coloring page, a word search and a maze.

How to print them

  1. Download the 4 PDFs right after checkout: English US Letter, English A4, Spanish US Letter and Spanish A4.
  2. Print at "Actual size". Use cardstock for bingo cards, place cards and thankful cards, and plain paper for trivia and Scattergories sheets.
  3. Cut, fold and play. No printer at home? Any copy shop can print it the same day.
